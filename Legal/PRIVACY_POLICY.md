# Privacy Policy

Updated November 2, 2026.

## Your computer and this device

Your computer runs the agents and stores workspace history and files. Connected devices may cache conversations, images and other files. Drafts and queued messages may also be stored on this device. Authorized remote traffic may travel through your configured relay or a Cloudflare tunnel.

## Accounts, providers and pairing

When you use an account, the configured account service processes identity and profile information, workspace membership, approved devices and connection records. Hosted sign-in uses WorkOS; the hosted API and managed tunnels use Cloudflare. Your computer connects to your chosen AI providers using your existing provider accounts; those providers receive the prompts, attachments and context required for the work you request under their own terms. Device approval and account ownership are independent of subscription access.

## Billing

Where subscriptions are enabled, Polar is the merchant of record and processes checkout, customer, payment and tax information. Synara associates your account identifier and email with the billing customer and stores subscription identifiers, access status, billing periods and billing-event identifiers. Card details entered at checkout are handled by Polar and its payment processor, not by Synara's billing backend. Billing records are separate from optional analytics.

## Usage sharing

When usage sharing is enabled and a receiver is configured, limited metadata can include app version, platform, feature names, provider and catalogued model categories, durations, token counts and connection outcomes. It excludes chat content, prompts, file contents and credentials. Signed-in events can include a pseudonymous account hash; it is not an anonymous identifier. You can turn sharing off in Settings. Hosted raw product events are retained for up to 30 days; separate release diagnostics have a different retention policy.

## Your controls and contact

You can revoke approved devices, sign out and request account deletion. These actions do not erase local project files or chat history on your computer. Turning off usage sharing clears unsent analytics on that installation; it cannot recall events already received. Billing providers may retain transaction records under their own legal obligations. For support or privacy requests, contact emandipietro@gmail.com. Self-hosted services may have their own privacy policies.
